Use WeekBloom with Claude

WeekBloom helps you plan a weekly schedule. The Claude connector can read and update the timetable data available to the account you authorize.

Connect

  1. Add WeekBloom from Claude's Connectors directory.
  2. Sign in to WeekBloom through the OAuth page and review the access request.
  3. Return to Claude and ask it to show your week schedule or find a free time slot.

What you can ask

  • View weekly schedules and list timetable events.
  • Find free time and check for overlapping events.
  • Create, update, and remove events in timetables you can access.
  • Review eligible shared timetable details and invitations.
  • Review Bloom Pet profiles and companion preferences.

Safety and access

The connector follows the permissions of the WeekBloom account you sign in with. Claude may ask you to confirm actions that change data. Review the action before approving it. Never share passwords, access tokens, or private credentials in a prompt or support request.

The connector does not accept payment details or initiate purchases. Paid subscriptions are handled separately in the WeekBloom Android app through Google Play.
